# Audience Playbook

Audiences are signals, exclusions, and reporting dimensions.

They are not a substitute for understanding the customer.

## Customer Lists

Use for:

- existing customer exclusions
- lapsed-customer campaigns
- value-based signals
- new-customer acquisition reporting
- retention or upsell targeting

Keep lists fresh and labeled by business meaning, not just upload date.

## Website Visitors

Use for:

- remarketing
- funnel-stage reporting
- excluding recent converters
- testing warm versus cold performance

Segment by meaningful behavior:

- product viewers
- cart abandoners
- checkout starters
- high-intent page visitors
- recent purchasers

## YouTube And Engagement Audiences

Use for:

- creator partnership video viewers
- channel engagement
- video-view retargeting
- building warm pools from useful content

## Audience Questions

Ask:

- Are existing customers excluded where acquisition is the goal?
- Are new customer results separated from returning buyers?
- Are warm audiences being over-credited?
- Are customer lists fresh enough to trust?
- Are audiences used as signals or hard targeting?
- Does the audience match the campaign job?

## Common Mistakes

- treating remarketing ROAS as incremental acquisition
- uploading one giant customer list with no business meaning
- using stale customer lists
- confusing audience observation with targeting
- letting PMax claim returning demand as new growth

## Agent Rule

Describe what the audience is supposed to do before reporting whether it
worked.
