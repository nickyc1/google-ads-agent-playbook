# Demand Gen And YouTube Playbook

Demand Gen and YouTube are useful when the account needs visual demand,
education, remarketing, or creator-led distribution.

They should not be judged exactly like bottom-of-funnel Search.

## Useful Jobs

- creator partnership videos
- product education
- remarketing
- customer proof
- hook testing
- short-form video testing
- broad demand creation
- warming audiences before Search or Shopping demand

## Creative Checks

For video assets, check:

- does the first two seconds say or show something useful?
- is the product visible?
- does the video feel native to the placement?
- is the claim specific?
- is the CTA clear?
- does the landing page continue the same promise?
- are different hooks being tested?

## Creator Videos

Creator videos can work because the messenger changes.

The same product and offer can perform differently when the ad appears to come
from a person the audience already trusts.

When evaluating creator videos, compare:

- creator organic baseline
- paid CTR
- view rate
- engaged-view conversions
- CPA or revenue
- comments and qualitative feedback
- performance against brand-made video

## Measurement Caveats

Watch:

- view-through influence
- engaged-view conversions
- conversion lag
- remarketing overlap
- small sample sizes
- creative fatigue

## Agent Rule

Do not declare a YouTube or Demand Gen campaign a failure only because it does
not match Search CPA immediately. Judge it against the job it was hired to do.
