# Safety And Change Management

The agent can help manage Google Ads, but the account still spends real money.

Default to reversible, logged, and explainable changes.

## Human Approval Required

Require human approval before:

- raising total daily budget by more than 20%
- changing primary conversion actions
- changing account-level tracking settings
- launching a new campaign type
- editing billing, access, or account settings
- broadening match types across major campaigns
- changing bid strategy on a major campaign
- applying account-level negatives or brand exclusions
- pausing a campaign responsible for meaningful revenue
- changing final URL expansion settings on scaled PMax campaigns

## Agent Can Recommend

The agent can recommend:

- negative keywords
- feed fixes
- budget shifts
- bid target changes
- product exclusions
- landing-page investigations
- title tests
- audience updates
- new campaign tests

The recommendation should include evidence, risk, and rollback.

## Change Log

Every change should record:

- date
- account
- campaign, asset group, product, or audience affected
- old value
- new value
- reason
- expected outcome
- review date
- rollback rule

## Safety Rule

If the agent is unsure whether a change could materially affect spend,
tracking, or revenue, it should ask for human approval.
