# Daily Optimization Workflow

Daily optimization is mostly triage.

The agent should separate real problems from normal noise and avoid creating
account churn just because numbers moved.

## Daily Checks

Run these every business day:

1. tracking health
2. spend pacing
3. conversion volume
4. CPA / ROAS movement
5. budget-limited campaigns
6. campaign or asset group anomalies
7. search term waste
8. product-level waste
9. Merchant Center disapprovals
10. landing-page or URL failures

## Action Types

### Pause or exclude

Use when spend crossed a defined threshold and the traffic is clearly
irrelevant or broken.

Examples:

- irrelevant search terms
- products disapproved or out of stock
- landing pages returning errors
- placements or terms that are brand-unsafe

### Budget shift

Use when one campaign is constrained and another is wasting or underspending.

Do not shift budget only because yesterday looked good. Confirm the pattern
over a meaningful comparison window.

### Feed fix

Use when product eligibility, title quality, labels, or page mismatch are
blocking profitable delivery.

### Bid strategy watch

Use when a bid strategy is in learning, constrained by budget, starved for
conversion volume, or optimizing toward the wrong conversion action.

### Human review

Use when the account needs taste, context, or business judgment.

Examples:

- promo economics
- margin tradeoffs
- brand risk
- creative quality
- customer fit
- whether a query is actually valuable

## Stop-Loss Defaults

These are starting points, not laws.

- Search query: review after 20 to 30 clicks with no conversion, sooner if
  obviously irrelevant.
- Product: review after 1.5x to 2x target CPA in spend without conversion.
- Campaign: review after 3x to 5x target CPA in spend without conversion, or
  sooner if tracking or traffic quality is suspect.
- New test: do not judge before the minimum impression, click, or conversion
  threshold unless something is broken.

## Rule

If the agent recommends a change, it must also say what it would need to see to
reverse that change.
