# Commerce Testing And Merchandising

Reviewed: 2026-10-08 (America/Chicago)

Five execution workflows that build on feed health, campaign structure, and
measurement. These are testable operating recommendations, not promised lifts.
Fix missing products, ineligible offers, and broken purchase tracking first.

## 1. Test The Shopping Product Page

Hypothesis: a product page that answers the buyer's main uncertainty can improve
purchase rate without changing the product, price, or traffic source.

Keep the exact advertised product and variant easy to identify and purchase.
Align the feed, page, structured data, price, availability, and checkout. A
generic article or quiz is not a substitute for a qualifying Shopping product
landing page. [Google's landing-page requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/4752265?hl=en).

Test one substantial improvement first: on-body fit evidence, a material demo,
clear bundle contents, a sizing explanation, or evidence addressing a frequent
objection. Keep the control's product and offer unchanged.

Use stable visitor assignment, consistent acquisition traffic, and purchase
rate or contribution per session as the primary outcome. Monitor AOV, refunds,
page speed, and feed eligibility. Define the sample and decision method before
launch; a before/after promotion comparison is not a randomized test.

## 2. Use Intent-Matched Page Templates

These are starting structures, not mandatory funnel steps. A clear offer for
an easy-to-understand product can send cold traffic directly to a strong PDP.

| Page | Section outline | Best initial use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product page | Exact product and buy action; primary benefit; demonstration; fit/specifications; verified customer proof; objections; shipping and returns | Shopping and product-led Search or Demand Gen |
| Comparison | Decision being made; disclosed publisher; evaluation criteria; dated evidence table; tradeoffs; who each option suits; product CTA | Relevant comparison Search and Demand Gen |
| Buying guide | Buyer situation; selection criteria; use cases; suitable options; who should not buy; route to relevant products | Educational Search and Demand Gen |
| Advertorial | Clear advertiser identity; relatable problem; explanation; supported evidence; product demonstration; limitations; offer and CTA | Educational Demand Gen concepts that need more context |

Do not impersonate an independent review site, invent rankings, fabricate
testimonials, or hide material limitations. Keep comparisons factual and
current. Test an intermediate page against direct-to-product traffic rather
than assuming more steps improve conversion. Generic editorial templates are
not blanket permission to use them as Shopping destinations.

## 3. Turn AI Max Discovery Into Reusable Intent

1. Define allowed products, brand boundaries, destination controls, budget, and
   purchase/value tracking before testing AI Max for Search.
2. Review served queries together with headlines and destination pages. Allow
   for conversion delay; keep brand, competitor, and category intent distinct.
3. Group recurring, economically useful intent. Record spend, purchases,
   value, observed new-customer evidence, sample size, and current page match.
4. Where volume and message specificity justify it, test a dedicated ad group,
   exact/phrase keywords, and a better-matched page. Do not restructure around
   a single lucky conversion or assume exact match guarantees query routing.
5. Translate the learning into truthful product titles, page language, image
   briefs, and video hooks. Reject attributes or benefits the product lacks.
6. Evaluate account-level results and overlap. Change negatives or URL rules
   deliberately; preserve a record of the discovery traffic being displaced.

Google documents query, asset, and destination reporting. Harvesting those
insights into other channels is our operating method, not a guaranteed feature
benefit. Reports also omit some low-volume queries.
[AI Max reporting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16470459?hl=en)
and [search-term reporting limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2472708?hl=en).

## 4. Build Stitched Videos With A Job For Each Clip

Use the following storyboard as a hypothesis, not a required video length:

| Sequence | Job | Example asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | Identify the buyer's situation quickly | Customer describing a real frustration |
| Explanation | Show why the product fits | Founder or knowledgeable creator |
| Proof | Demonstrate rather than repeat a claim | Product use, close-up, or substantiated testimonial |
| Objection | Answer the main hesitation | Sizing, care, price-per-item, or materials explanation |
| CTA | Give a clear next action | Actual product, current offer, matching page |

Keep speakers' statements in context. Secure paid usage, editing, likeness,
music, territory, and term rights for the actual placements. Meta permission
alone is not evidence of YouTube usage rights. Never manufacture customer
experience or use AI to invent product performance.

Create a coherent master, then deliberate horizontal and vertical versions
with readable captions and safe framing. Compare hook variants while holding
the body and offer constant; distinguish a new concept from a recut.
See the [Demand Gen execution workflow](demand-gen/README.md).

## 5. Audit Merchandising Beyond Feed Errors

An approved feed can still sell an uncompetitive or confusing offer.

| Audit area | Check | Owner/output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shipping | Real threshold, cost, delivery estimate, exceptions; agreement across ads, feed, site, and checkout | Operations + media: corrected facts, proposed economic tests |
| Returns | Window, exclusions, fees, instructions, and Merchant Center policy consistency | Operations: verified policy |
| Reviews | Authenticity, coverage, useful product proof, eligibility for relevant rating programs | Ecommerce: coverage gaps, no promised stars |
| Promotions | Valid dates, price history where relevant, bundle contents, coupon usability, margin | Merchandising: tested offer and rollback |
| Store Quality | Available diagnostics, shipping/returns experience, missing data and eligibility | Merchant Center owner: prioritized fixes |

Store Quality benefits depend on Google's criteria and the market. Correcting
data does not guarantee a badge, ranking improvement, or sales lift.
[Store Quality documentation](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14261098?hl=en).

Do not invent a lower shipping threshold to improve an ad. Business policy
changes need approval and a margin calculation, not just a feed edit.

## Execution Gate

Use the [experiment plan](../templates/experiment-plan.md): hypothesis, owner,
control, treatment, primary metric, sample, conversion lag, maximum loss,
approval, and rollback. Distinguish risk-based early stops from evidence that
a treatment won. Store the result even when inconclusive.
