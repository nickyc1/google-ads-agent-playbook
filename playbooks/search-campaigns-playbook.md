# Search Campaigns Playbook

Search is still the cleanest place to see demand.

It is also where broad match, automation, brand leakage, and weak conversion
signals can quietly waste money.

## Search Campaign Jobs

Use Search for:

- brand protection
- high-intent non-brand demand
- competitor or alternative searches when economics work
- category demand
- problem-aware queries
- landing-page and message tests
- query mining

## AI Max For Search

AI Max is a Search automation layer for scaling and intent discovery.

Use the dedicated [AI Max for Search playbook](ai-max-search-playbook.md) when
you are considering it.

Short version:

- Broad match is a keyword match type.
- AI Max is a suite of AI-powered Search features.
- It can expand matching, customize text, and optionally expand final URLs.
- It needs Smart Bidding and clean conversion data.
- It is best tested on campaigns that already work.
- It should be reviewed through AI Max search term, headline, and landing-page
  reporting.

## Brand Versus Non-Brand

Separate brand and non-brand reporting.

Brand search can be useful, but it should not make prospecting look better than
it is.

Ask:

- Is this capturing demand we created elsewhere?
- Are we overpaying for branded clicks?
- Is branded search protecting against competitors or just taking credit?
- Are brand conversions being mixed into non-brand decisions?

## Match Types

### Exact

Use when control matters and query intent is tightly defined.

### Phrase

Use for controlled expansion around known intent.

### Broad

Use when:

- conversion tracking is clean
- negatives are maintained
- the account has enough conversion data
- landing pages match query intent
- the budget can absorb learning

Broad match without good measurement is just expensive guessing.

## Search Term Review

Review:

- irrelevant queries
- low-intent research queries
- competitor terms
- geographic mismatch
- support or login queries
- job seeker queries
- queries that indicate a different product
- high-spend no-conversion terms

## Agent Rule

Do not add negatives only because a query did not convert once. Check intent,
spend, click volume, match type, and whether the query could matter higher in
the funnel.
