# YouTube Creator Partnerships Playbook

Creator partnership ads work because the messenger changes.

The same product, same offer, and same landing page can feel completely
different when the ad comes from a person the customer already trusts instead
of a brand account.

Google calls this Creator partnerships boost.

The agent's job is not to create fake creator videos. The job is to help the
operator find the right real people, request the right access, structure the
test, and compare creator videos against normal brand creative.

## When To Use This

Use YouTube creator partnerships when:

- the product needs demonstration
- the product needs trust
- the audience already learns from creators
- the brand has customers, partners, founders, operators, or educators who can
  explain the product better than a brand ad
- the account needs more native short-form video
- brand videos are too polished or too generic
- Demand Gen, YouTube, or PMax needs better video inputs

Do not use it only because "creator ads are hot."

Use it because the person has a real reason to be trusted by the audience.

## Trust Persona

A customer persona answers:

```text
Who buys this?
```

A trust persona answers:

```text
Who does that customer listen to before they buy?
```

That person may be:

- a customer with a small but relevant audience
- a founder in the category
- an operator who uses the product
- a practitioner with credibility
- a reviewer with real taste
- an educator the audience already watches
- a niche creator with a better fit than a bigger creator

The best creator is not always the biggest creator.

The best creator is the person whose recommendation lowers the customer's
guard.

## Access Setup

The exact interface can move around, but the operating path is:

1. Link the brand YouTube channel to Google Ads.
2. In Google Ads, go to Tools > Data Manager > YouTube.
3. Confirm the brand channel is linked.
4. Enable the permissions needed for reporting, remarketing, and engagement.
5. Ask the creator to upload the video as public or unlisted.
6. Do not use private videos.
7. Request access to the creator video from Google Ads.
8. Have the creator approve the request.
9. Confirm the video appears with the right permissions.
10. Use the approved creator video inside the relevant campaign.

The approved permissions usually matter more than the upload itself.

Look for:

- video performance
- remarketing
- engagement
- Creator partnership boost

If the account cannot see performance or build audiences, the setup is not
complete.

## Creator Upload Rules

Ask for separate uploads when you need different formats.

Good minimum set:

- vertical short
- square or feed cut
- horizontal cut if YouTube in-stream is part of the plan

Each cut should be uploaded as its own video. Do not rely on one creative
version to do every job.

## Creator Brief

The brief should protect clarity without killing the creator's voice.

Give the creator:

- product
- audience
- main customer pain
- offer
- landing page
- claims they can and cannot make
- required disclosure language
- one CTA
- examples of angles that have worked
- examples of angles that feel too brand-polished

Then let them talk like themselves.

Minimum creative requirements:

1. Say or show the product in the first one to two seconds.
2. Show the product naturally.
3. Explain the use case in plain language.
4. Make one clear claim.
5. Include the required disclosure.
6. End with one CTA.

Bad brief:

```text
Please read this script exactly.
```

Better brief:

```text
Here is the customer, the problem, the offer, and the claim we can prove. Say
it how you would actually explain it to your audience.
```

## Rights And Terms

Do not start running creator ads without usage terms.

Document:

- paid usage window
- approved platforms
- approved ad accounts
- whether organic reposting is allowed
- whether raw footage is included
- whether edits are allowed
- whether whitelisting / creator partnership boosting is allowed
- exclusivity, if any
- revocation process
- disclosure requirements

Creators usually retain ownership of the video and can remove access. Build
that into the operating plan.

## Campaign Uses

Creator partnership videos can be tested in:

- Demand Gen
- YouTube Video Reach
- YouTube Video View
- Performance Max when video assets are useful
- remarketing sequences
- product education sequences
- launch or promo campaigns

The same creator video can have different jobs depending on placement.

A short vertical demo may be a prospecting hook in Demand Gen and a retargeting
proof point in another campaign.

## Test Design

Do not compare one creator video against the whole account.

Compare it against a fair baseline.

Possible baselines:

- brand video with similar offer
- founder video
- product demo
- static image ad
- existing top-performing YouTube creative
- creator's own organic performance

Minimum test notes:

- video URL
- creator
- audience fit
- placement
- campaign
- spend
- impressions
- views
- view rate
- CTR
- CPC
- conversions
- CPA
- revenue or profit
- engaged-view conversions
- comments or qualitative feedback

If the sample is tiny, call it directional.

## What To Watch

Creator ads can look good for the wrong reason.

Watch for:

- remarketing overlap
- brand demand capture
- conversion lag
- view-through inflation
- weak landing-page match
- small sample size
- novelty effect
- creator fatigue
- comment quality

The agent should separate a real creator signal from attribution noise.

## AI Agent Workflow

Use AI to operate the system around real people.

The agent can:

1. Search customer lists for people with audiences.
2. Search social channels for creators in the category.
3. Score creators by trust fit, not follower count alone.
4. Review the creator's last 10-20 posts.
5. Pull median views and engagement.
6. Identify whether the creator actually talks to the ICP.
7. Draft outreach.
8. Track creator status.
9. Track video access status.
10. Compare creator video performance against brand video.
11. Summarize what angles creators found that the brand missed.

The last one matters.

Creators often find the angle the brand team would never write in a brief.

## Creator Scorecard

Use a simple scorecard before outreach.

| Area | Question |
|---|---|
| Audience fit | Does this person's audience look like the buyer or trust persona? |
| Credibility | Has this person earned permission to talk about the category? |
| Content quality | Can they explain clearly on video? |
| Median reach | Do normal posts get enough views to prove baseline attention? |
| Comment quality | Do real people ask real buying questions? |
| Brand fit | Would the brand be comfortable appearing next to this person? |
| Product fit | Can this creator use or understand the product naturally? |
| Paid fit | Would their content still work with media spend behind it? |

Follower count is a filter, not the strategy.

## Recommended First Test

Start small.

1. Pick one product or offer.
2. Find three to five creators with strong trust fit.
3. Get one to two videos per creator.
4. Request video access.
5. Launch creator videos against a comparable brand video.
6. Keep budget capped until signal appears.
7. Review after enough impressions, clicks, and conversions to make a real
   decision.
8. Scale the winning creator, angle, or format.
9. Turn learnings into the next creator brief.

The goal is not one viral video.

The goal is a repeatable creator acquisition and testing system.

## Agent Rule

Do not recommend creator partnership spend only because a creator has a large
audience.

Explain why the audience trusts that person, what job the video is doing, what
baseline it should beat, and what result would justify scaling.
