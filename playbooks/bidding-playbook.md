# Bidding Playbook

Bidding is not magic.

Bid strategy works when the account gives Google the right conversion signal,
enough data, and a business goal that matches the economics.

## Bid Strategies

### Manual CPC

Useful for:

- small tests
- tight control
- early search campaigns
- accounts without enough conversion data

Risk:

- easy to overbid or underbid manually
- does not use auction-time signals fully

### Maximize Clicks

Useful for:

- early traffic collection
- low-risk learning
- accounts where conversion tracking is not ready

Risk:

- optimizes for clicks, not quality

### Maximize Conversions

Useful for:

- conversion volume when CPA target is flexible
- campaigns with clean primary conversions

Risk:

- can overspend if conversion quality is weak
- can chase cheap low-value conversions

### Target CPA

Useful for:

- lead gen or fixed-value conversion goals
- campaigns with stable conversion volume

Risk:

- target too tight can starve volume
- target too loose can waste spend

### Maximize Conversion Value

Useful for:

- ecommerce with revenue values
- accounts optimizing for value, not just order count

Risk:

- values may not reflect margin, refunds, or new-customer quality

### Target ROAS

Useful for:

- ecommerce accounts with enough conversion value
- scaling within an efficiency guardrail

Risk:

- ROAS targets can protect efficiency while starving growth
- in-platform ROAS may not equal profit

## Before Changing Bids

Check:

- primary conversion actions
- conversion volume by campaign
- conversion lag
- value tracking accuracy
- budget limits
- learning status
- seasonality or promo context
- recent large account changes
- whether the target matches business economics

## Change Rules

- Do not change bid targets daily.
- Avoid stacking bid changes with budget, landing-page, feed, and creative
  changes unless the account is broken.
- Move targets gradually unless the current setup is clearly wrong.
- Record the previous target, new target, reason, date, and rollback condition.

## Agent Rule

Do not recommend a bid strategy change until you explain whether the problem is
bidding, measurement, feed quality, landing page, budget, or traffic quality.
