# Merchant Center And Feed Playbook

For ecommerce Google Ads, the feed is the campaign.

The agent should inspect Merchant Center and product data before assuming the
problem is bidding or budgets.

## Daily Feed Checks

- account-level warnings
- product disapprovals
- item issues on revenue-driving products
- price mismatch
- availability mismatch
- broken image links
- missing required attributes
- stale feed fetches
- product count changes
- destination eligibility

## Weekly Feed Checks

- product titles
- product descriptions
- product type taxonomy
- Google product categories
- custom labels
- GTIN, MPN, and brand quality
- image coverage
- promo eligibility
- stale or duplicate products
- live store products missing from Merchant Center
- Merchant Center products unavailable on site

## Custom Labels

Maintain labels for:

- product family
- margin tier
- price tier
- sales tier
- inventory health
- seasonality
- promo state
- campaign role
- test status

Labels make reporting and product-level control possible. They do not improve
performance by themselves.

## Product Titles

Good titles usually lead with the language shoppers use.

Test title formulas by product family. Do not apply one universal formula to
the whole catalog.

Common title elements:

- product type
- use case
- material
- color
- size
- pack quantity
- gender or fit when relevant
- brand when it adds trust or differentiation

## Landing-Page Match

For important products, check:

- Does the page hero match the feed-title angle?
- Does the page support the search intent implied by the ad?
- Does the page clearly show price, availability, size, color, shipping,
  returns, reviews, and pack contents?
- Does the page handle the buyer's main objection before the CTA?

## Agent Rule

Before recommending a campaign change for Shopping or PMax, confirm whether
the affected products are eligible, in stock, correctly labeled, and aligned
with the landing page.
