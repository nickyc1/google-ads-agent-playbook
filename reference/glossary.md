# Glossary

## Brand

Traffic from people searching for the company, product, or branded terms.

## Non-Brand

Traffic from category, problem, competitor, or use-case searches that do not
include the brand.

## PMax

Performance Max, a Google Ads campaign type that can serve across multiple
Google properties using campaign goals, assets, feeds, audiences, and
conversion signals.

## Merchant Center

The product-data layer for Shopping ads and many ecommerce Google Ads
workflows.

## Product Group

The Standard Shopping structure used to subdivide, include, exclude, and bid on
products using feed attributes.

## Listing Group

The PMax structure used to include or exclude product subsets.

## Custom Label

A Merchant Center feed attribute used by advertisers to classify products by
business logic, such as margin tier, product family, promo state, or campaign
role.

## Primary Conversion Action

The conversion action Google Ads uses for bidding and optimization.

## Secondary Conversion Action

A conversion action used for observation or reporting, not primary bidding.

## Conversion Lag

The delay between ad interaction and recorded conversion.

## View-Through Conversion

A conversion attributed after someone saw an ad but did not click it.

## Engaged-View Conversion

A conversion attributed after a meaningful video engagement.
